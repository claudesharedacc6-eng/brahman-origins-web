Game Guidelines

How Brahman Origins works

Eight steps, one growing character. Here's what happens between signing in and holding the Loyalty Award.

01

Sign in with UB Mail

Use Login with UB Mail and pick your official @ub.edu.ph Google account. Only UB Mail accounts save progress and play the minigames. No account? Continue as Guest to tour the campuses.

02

Launch the game

Press Play. The game runs in your browser and shares your account with this website. The first load downloads the campuses and can take a few minutes. After that, your browser keeps a copy and it starts much faster. Use a laptop or desktop with Chrome or Edge for the best experience.

03

Choose your campus and character

Open Campus Selection from the main menu and pick one of the four UB campuses: UB Elementary, UB Main, UB Millennium or UB Lipa. Then choose a boy or girl student. Choose carefully: your character stays with you and grows up as you move through the year levels.

04

Arrive on campus

You start at the campus entrance. Look around, then talk to the people you meet: they welcome you, tell you about the campus, and point you to your first quest.

05

Complete quests and minigames

Teacher NPCs hand out quests for each year level. Every quest leads to an educational minigame. Finish it to earn XP and move up to the next level. There's no skipping a year.

06

Learn the controls

  • W A S DMove your character
  • MouseLook around
  • SpaceJump
  • ETalk to NPCs and interact with objects
  • Left AltFree the mouse pointer (to click buttons)
  • EscGo back in menus
07

Track your progress

Open My Progress on this website to see your year level and XP on each campus, and your place on each campus leaderboard. Found a bug? Report it there and the team will fix it.

08

Earn the Loyalty Award

Finish your campus's final year and graduate. The Loyalty Award is the one reward reserved for players who saw the whole journey through.

Know the steps. Now walk them.
