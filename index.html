About Brahman Origins

Every UBian's education, turned into a world worth exploring.

Most of us only get to live our school years once. Brahman Origins lets players replay that path as a game: starting in Elementary, working through High School and Senior High, and arriving, quest by quest, at College. NPCs modeled on the mentors who shape a UB education hand out quests and minigames tied to real subjects, and everything you earn stays with one character for the whole journey.

This website is the portal around that game: where players sign in, launch the game, check their progress, and where administrators keep the world running.
